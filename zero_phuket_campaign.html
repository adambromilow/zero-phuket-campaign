Phuket is not one market. Bang Tao, Nai Yang, Laguna, Rawai, Kamala — they all attract different buyers. The question is not just 'Should I buy in Phuket?' — it's 'Which area fits my goal?'

05Cheapest condo ≠ best deal
▾
CTA: SMART
Hook
"The cheapest condo is not always the best deal."
Script
A good deal depends on the area, rental demand, number of units, the developer, payment plan, and how easy it is to sell later. Price matters — but the full story matters more.
Visuals
Big text: Cheap price ≠ good deal
Checklist: Area / Rental / Units / Developer / Resale
Lead magnet
Buyer checklist
06Rent against 300 owners?
▾
CTA: BOUTIQUE
Hook
"Would you want to rent against 300 other owners?"
Script
In a very big project, many owners rent similar units at the same time — same size, same view, same furniture. Then people compete on price. A boutique project has fewer units and a clearer identity.
Visuals
Graphic of many identical listings, then boutique render contrast
Lead magnet
Boutique vs big condo comparison
07ROI can look good on paper
▾
CTA: ROI
Hook
"ROI can look good on paper. But what is behind it?"
Script
How much can the unit rent for? How often will it be rented? What are the costs? Who manages it? Can the owner use it? The number only matters when the assumptions are clear.
Visuals
iPad/calculator, simple text: Rent / Occupancy / Costs / Management
Lead magnet
Simple rental model

"Before a buyer reserves, this is what I want them to understand."
Script
What exactly are you buying? Who is developing it? How do the payments work? What ownership type fits you? And what is the rental plan? If those answers are clear, the decision becomes much easier.

10What if you don't sell?
▾
CTA: SCENARIO
Hook
"What if you don't sell when you planned?"
Script
This is a question every property investor should ask. Can the unit still rent? Is the area still attractive? Is the payment plan comfortable? Can you hold it longer if needed?
Visuals
Simple board: Sell / Rent / Hold — calm tone, not fear-based

Investor scenario plan
